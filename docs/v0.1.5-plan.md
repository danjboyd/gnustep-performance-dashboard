the SDK still has the upstream leak. No shipped source or runtime was changed.

General conservative Leaks limitations are already documented in
[architecture.md](architecture.md#conservative-leak-detection-limits).

of eight deliberate leaks in two x86_64 runs, with clean traces and zero
drops. Diagnostic runs explained the misses through pointer-like stale or
uninitialized words in retained heap tails. This is a general conservative
reachability limitation, not proof of an arm64-specific failure. The possible
heap-word-retention reporting idea remains unimplemented; no detector or
fixture semantics changed in this preparation.

The existing arm64 record supports one short, single-thread Allocations smoke
case: supplied text reports 442.49 ms, 49,145 allocations, 2,922 stacks and
